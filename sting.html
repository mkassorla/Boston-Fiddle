Behind the Saybrook Tavern, After Dark

The Sting Is Not Yet Sprung

You have named the mode, and named it rightly — but the trap is not yet baited to Madam’s satisfaction. Samuel waits in the shadows with the fiddle. The keeper counts his silver and dreams of thirty pieces of eight.

Return when your instructor gives the word. This very page will carry you into the Sting when Madam springs it.
